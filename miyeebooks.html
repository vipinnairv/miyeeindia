MiyeeBooks

A complete MSME accounting suite that runs in your browser, with no installation. Built to help the MSME business community grow.

🇮🇳 Your books are stored on Google Cloud servers in Mumbai, India (asia-south1), encrypted in transit and at rest, and handled in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

One suite for your business

From the shop counter to the bank

MiyeeBooks brings your books, GST, payments, stock and lender papers together, so the numbers you enter once work for everything else.

  • Invoices, purchases and UPI collections posted to the books for you
  • GST returns, TDS and payroll from the same entries
  • Stock, billing counter and supplier payments
  • Credit score, CMA data and bank papers ready for banks and NBFCs
