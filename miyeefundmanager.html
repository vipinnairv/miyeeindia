FREE · FUND MANAGEMENT

MiyeeFundManager

A fund manager workbench for SEBI regulated funds. Free to use, to help the MSME business community grow.

Launch app ↗ View on GitHub ↗
Who it is for

Built for SEBI regulated funds

AIFs

Category I, II and III Alternative Investment Funds, including large value funds, accredited investor only schemes and angel funds.

Mutual fund schemes

Scheme holdings and trades, NAV, subscriptions and redemptions with exit load, and an unrealised appreciation reserve.

Portfolio Management Services

PMS client portfolios, with each client's own holdings, FIFO cost, gains and cash.

Fund operations

Investors, capital and NAV

Investors and KYC

Investor records and KYC, capital commitments and drawdowns, and investor statements.

Subscriptions and redemptions

Process subscriptions and redemptions with exit load, backed by a cash ledger.

NAV and fees

NAV with saved prices and a re-pricing check for every past NAV, plus management fees.

Fund accounting

Double entry, end to end

Books of account

Journal, ledger, trial balance, statement of net assets, revenue account and NAV reconciliation.

Control and flexibility

Manual journals and accruals, editable chart of accounts, posting rules, reclassification and an audit trail.

Portfolio and compliance

Documents, checks and analysis

SEBI documents and checks

SEBI investor documents and compliance checks.

Startup portfolio analysis

Funding rounds, pre and post money valuation, fund stake, fair value marks, MOIC, IRR, TVPI and DPI.

Access and data

Controls and portability

Sign-in and approvals

Optional sign-in with Admin, Maker, Checker and Viewer roles, maker-checker approvals and a full activity log.

Your data

Single file web app. Data is stored in your browser, with JSON backup and optional Google Drive sync.

Try it with sample data

Open Data Management and click Load Sample Data to load 8 schemes, 25 investors, 43 portfolio companies and 18 months of activity (Apr 2025 to Sep 2026). All names and numbers are fictitious.
