About this site

MiyeeIndia Tech Labs

MiyeeIndia Tech Labs is a personal, non-commercial initiative. It brings together free tools and free learning resources for the MSME business community: accounting, stock analysis, personal finance dashboards, PDF tools, task management and fund accounting, plus guides on GST, income tax, accounting standards and markets.

Everything here is offered free of charge. This site does not sell services, take payments or solicit clients. It is built in my personal capacity, and the views and content are my own and do not represent any employer.

How the tools are built

Domain-led finance automation

My contribution is the domain side. I write the product specification, design the user flow, encode the tax and accounting logic (which slabs apply, which sections trigger and which reconciliations must happen), and test the output against the statute and against real cases I have handled. I use AI tools such as Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT as the engineering partner for code generation and syntax.
