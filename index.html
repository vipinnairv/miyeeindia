About this site

MiyeeIndia Tech Labs

MiyeeIndia Tech Labs is my personal portfolio and a home for the free resources I create. It brings together browser-based apps and tools for accounting, tax, investing, document handling, planning and learning, alongside in-depth guides on GST, income tax, accounting standards and the markets.

The site is non-commercial. Nothing here is sold, no payments are taken and no clients are solicited. It is built in my personal capacity, and the views and content are my own and do not represent any employer.

How the tools are built

Domain-led finance automation

Every tool starts with a real problem I have met in practice. I write the specification, design the user flow and encode the tax and accounting logic: which slabs apply, which sections are triggered and which reconciliations must hold. I then test the results against the statute and against real cases before anything is published.

AI tools such as Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT act as my engineering partner for code generation, which lets me turn deep domain knowledge into working software quickly and keep the focus on getting the finance right.

Experience

Experience in brief

Over 14 years in finance, accounts and taxation, spanning a technology business incubator, an IT services company, a digital asset exchange with compliance across India, Singapore and Australia, my own accounting and compliance practice for SMEs, and articleship with a Chartered Accountant firm.

The work covers month-end and year-end closing, statutory audits, GST, TDS and income tax compliance, FCRA and grant reporting, MIS and budgeting, and ERP implementation and automation.
