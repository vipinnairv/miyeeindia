Who we are

Finance expertise, turned into software

Every product starts with a real problem met in practice: closing the books, filing GST, reconciling TDS, tracking cash runway, valuing a business or handling sensitive documents.

Our mission

Give every Indian business, however small, the finance and compliance tools that large companies take for granted, at a price that makes sense for an MSME.

What we build

Accounting and GST (MiyeeBooks), CFO dashboards, PDF tools, fund accounting, stock analysis, tax calculators, financial modelling, task management and an upskilling platform.

How we build

Domain experts write the tax and accounting logic, and test it against the statute and real cases. Apps run in your browser, keep data in your control and need no installation.

What we stand for

Our principles

Correct first

Tax slabs, sections and reconciliations are checked against the law before a feature ships.

Your data, your control

Apps work in your browser, with your own backups and optional cloud sync. We follow the DPDP Act.

Fair, simple pricing

Core features stay free. Paid plans are clearly priced, with an invoice for every payment and no lock-in.

Built for India

GST, TDS, Income-tax Act, 2025, SEBI and ICAI requirements are built in, not bolted on.
