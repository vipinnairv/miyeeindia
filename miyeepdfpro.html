FREE · PDF TOOLS

MiyeePDFPro

An Acrobat-class PDF suite that runs entirely in your browser. No file is ever uploaded: every operation happens locally on your own device. Built to help the MSME business community grow.

Edit Text

Replaces the original text rather than covering it, reusing the document's own font where possible.

Fill and Sign

Draw, type or upload a signature; place text, dates and check marks; fill and flatten form fields.

Find and Replace

Search and replace inside a PDF, keeping font, size and colour.

Check figures

Photos to PDF

Turn pictures of paper into one tidy document.

Pages

Find Sensitive Data

Finds PAN, GSTIN, Aadhaar, IFSC, card and account numbers and more, then redacts or masks them permanently.

Redact and Sanitize

Privacy

Local by design
