PRIVACY · DPDP

Privacy notice

How this site and the Miyee apps handle personal data, written with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) in mind.

This website

  • There are no accounts, sign-up forms, advertising or analytics on this site, and it does not set tracking cookies.
  • Your light or dark theme choice is saved only in your own browser.
  • Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on Cloudflare. As with any website, these providers may process your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages.
  • If you email me, your email address and message are used only to reply to you.

The Miyee apps

  • Most Miyee apps run in your browser and keep your data on your own device, in your browser's storage.
  • Some apps offer optional features such as sign-in, team access or backup to your own Google Drive. Where you use them, the data goes to that service under its own terms.
  • Each app explains its own data handling. Please read it before entering personal or client data.

Your choices and contact

  • You can clear any data an app has stored by clearing that site's data in your browser.
  • For privacy questions or requests, write to support@miyeetechlabs.com (or miyee.india@gmail.com).

This notice is general information, not legal advice. Last updated: October 2026.
