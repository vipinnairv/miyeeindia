FREE APPS FOR MSMEs

Tools that just work

Six independent tools, each built for a specific job. All of them are free to use, built to help the MSME business community grow. Pick one and launch it in a new tab.

Free Accounting

MiyeeBooks

A complete MSME accounting suite that runs entirely in your browser, covering GST, TDS, payroll, inventory and financial statements for small businesses in India.

  • Vouchers, chart of accounts, parties and cost centres
  • GST registers and returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-2B), TDS and payroll
  • Inventory, BOM and production, financial statements, MIS and ratios
  • No installation: data stays in your browser, with JSON or Google Drive backup

Best for: MSME owners, accountants and bookkeepers

Free Stock Analysis

MyeeStockBuddy

Techno-fundamental stock analysis using the R.I.S.E. framework, with a built-in portfolio manager. Pattern recognition for Indian equity markets, plus US and crypto.

  • Live prices, candlestick and line charts for Indian, US and crypto markets
  • Entry, exit and stop-loss signals with Fibonacci, Bollinger Bands, SMA, ATR and support/resistance
  • Portfolio manager for equity, crypto, mutual funds and FD/bonds with P&L tracking
  • No login needed; runs in your browser with JSON export and import

Best for: Individual investors and traders

Free CFO Dashboard

MiyeeCFO

CFO-level financial intelligence in a secure workspace: dashboards and analytical tools for net worth, cash runway, debt, budgets and goals, to support strategic finance decisions.

  • Net fund balance across bank, cash, FD and digital wallets
  • CFO metrics: savings rate, burn rate, fund runway and a 0-100 health score
  • Credit card tracker, loan and debt optimizer (Avalanche or Snowball)
  • Budget vs actual, savings goals, AI advisor and optional Google Drive sync

Best for: Individuals and professionals who want CFO-style control of their finances

Free PDF Tools

MiyeePDFPro

A professional PDF suite that runs entirely in your browser. Convert, extract and transform documents with speed and precision, and no file is ever uploaded.

  • Edit text, fill and sign, merge, split, reorder and compare
  • Real redaction, AES-256 password protection and sensitive data finder (PAN, GSTIN, Aadhaar)
  • OCR, and export to Word, Excel, text and images
  • Batch tools, figure checking and digital signature verification

Best for: Chartered accountants, finance and legal teams, and anyone handling documents

Free Task & Project Management

Miyee Task Manager Pro

A comprehensive task and project management web app to track personal goals or manage team collaboration, with a dashboard, calendar, goals, projects and an admin console.

  • Personal mode and Company/Team mode with roles and invite codes
  • Tasks with status, priority, tags, subtasks, recurring tasks and comments
  • Short, medium and long-term goals broken into projects
  • Export to Excel and calendar files; two-way Google Calendar sync

Free Fund Management

MiyeeFundManager

A fund manager workbench for SEBI regulated funds: AIF Category I, II and III, mutual fund schemes and Portfolio Management Services.

  • Investors and KYC, commitments and drawdowns, subscriptions and redemptions
  • Double entry fund accounting, NAV with re-pricing check, fees and investor statements
  • PMS client portfolios, startup portfolio analysis (MOIC, IRR, TVPI, DPI)
  • Maker-checker roles, audit trail, JSON backup and optional Google Drive sync

Best for: fund managers, fund accountants and compliance teams
