Illustration 83: Health insurance for parents

Suresh (45) pays Rs. 28,000 premium for his family plus Rs. 4,000 for a health check up, and Rs. 55,000 premium for his senior citizen parents.

Family: limit Rs. 25,000 (the check up allowance of Rs. 5,000 sits inside it). Allowed Rs. 25,000. Parents: limit Rs. 50,000. Allowed Rs. 50,000. Total under section 126: Rs. 75,000 (old regime only).

Illustration 84: Additional employee cost

A company taxed under section 200 hires 20 new employees in Tax Year 2026-27, each earning Rs. 20,000 a month and employed for more than 240 days, with salary paid through bank. Additional employee cost = Rs. 48 lakh. Deduction under section 146 = 30% = Rs. 14,40,000 this year and in each of the next two years.

Illustration 85: Donation with 10% cap

Gross total income Rs. 10,00,000, including LTCG of Rs. 2,00,000. Deductions under section 123 of Rs. 1,50,000. Donation of Rs. 1,20,000 to an approved trust (category D).

Adjusted GTI = 10,00,000 less 2,00,000 less 1,50,000 = Rs. 6,50,000. Qualifying limit = 10% = Rs. 65,000. Qualifying amount = lower of Rs. 1,20,000 and Rs. 65,000 = Rs. 65,000. Deduction at 50% = Rs. 32,500.

16.5 Section 134 (old 80GG): rent without HRA

For a self employed person or an employee who does not receive HRA, and who does not own a house at the place of work, the deduction is the least of: Rs. 5,000 a month; 25% of adjusted total income; and rent paid less 10% of adjusted total income.

Illustration 86: Rent deduction

Adjusted total income Rs. 4,80,000; rent paid Rs. 1,20,000. Least of Rs. 60,000; Rs. 1,20,000 (25%); and Rs. 72,000 (1,20,000 less 48,000) = Rs. 60,000.

16.6 Section 146 (old 80JJAA): conditions

  • Available to a business liable to tax audit; not to a business formed by splitting up or reconstruction, or on transfer of an existing business.
  • Additional employee: monthly emoluments up to Rs. 25,000, employed for 240 days or more in the year (150 days for apparel, footwear and leather), paid through banking channels, and participating in a recognised provident fund.
  • Deduction: 30% of additional employee cost for 3 years, starting from the year of employment. If the employee completes the required days only in the next year, the deduction starts from that year.
  • Accountant's report must be filed before the return due date.

16.7 Sections 127 and 154: disability

Section 154 (old 80U) is for the taxpayer who is a person with disability; section 127 (old 80DD) is for maintenance and medical treatment of a disabled dependant. Deduction is Rs. 75,000 for disability of 40% or more, and Rs. 1,25,000 for severe disability of 80% or more, on the basis of a certificate from a prescribed medical authority. Both are flat deductions, not linked to actual expenditure.

Key point

Profit-linked deductions are computed as if the eligible business were the only business of the assessee, and require an accountant's report before the return due date. The deduction is allowed only if claimed in a return filed by the due date.

Chapter summary

• Most deductions are available only in the old regime.

• Section 123 limit Rs. 1.5 lakh; section 124 adds Rs. 50,000 own NPS; employer NPS in both regimes.

• Section 133 donations have four categories and a 10% cap for some.

• Business deductions such as section 146 and profit-linked deductions have specific conditions and reports.
