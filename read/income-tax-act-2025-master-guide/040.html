37. Advance Tax, Interest and Fees

37.1 Advance tax (sections 403 to 411)

Payable if estimated tax after TDS and TCS is Rs. 10,000 or more. A resident senior citizen without business or professional income is exempt.

Illustration 141: Shortfall

Rohit, a freelancer, has assessed tax of Rs. 2,00,000 but paid only Rs. 50,000 in advance tax. Since he paid less than 90%, section 424 (old 234B) applies.

He pays the balance with his return on 31 July 2027. Interest = 1% x 1,50,000 x 4 months (April to July) = Rs. 6,000, plus interest under section 425 for missed instalments.

37.3 Computing advance tax and interest under section 425

Illustration 142: Instalments and interest

Section 424 does not apply because Rs. 3,90,000 is more than 90% of the assessed tax. The balance of Rs. 10,000 plus interest must be paid as self assessment tax before filing the return.

37.4 Points to remember

  • Estimated tax for advance tax purposes is after deducting TDS and TCS expected during the year.
  • Capital gains, winnings and dividends that arise after an instalment date are not counted for the earlier instalments if the remaining instalments are paid after they arise.
  • Interest is rounded: shortfall is rounded down to the nearest multiple of Rs. 100, and a fraction of a month counts as a full month for sections 423 and 424.
  • Companies must also take MAT into account for advance tax.

Chapter summary

• Payable if tax is Rs. 10,000 or more; instalments of 15%, 45%, 75% and 100%.

• Interest under sections 423, 424 and 425 at 1% a month.

• Late return fee Rs. 5,000 (Rs. 1,000 for small incomes).
