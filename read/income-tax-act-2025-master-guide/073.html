Figure

Nil up to 2.5 lakh; 5% to 5 lakh; 20% to 10 lakh; 30% above

Rebate (section 156)

New: up to Rs. 60,000 if income up to Rs. 12 lakh; Old: up to Rs. 12,500 if income up to Rs. 5 lakh

Standard deduction (salary)

New Rs. 75,000; Old Rs. 50,000

Surcharge (individuals)

10% above 50 lakh; 15% above 1 crore; 25% above 2 crore; 37% above 5 crore (old regime only)

Cess

4%

20%

12.5% above Rs. 1,25,000

12.5% (20% with indexation option for pre-23 July 2024 land and building of resident individuals and HUFs)

Company rates

25% or 30% (normal); 22% (section 200); 15% (section 201); foreign company 35%

MAT

14% of book profit; final tax from Tax Year 2026-27

30%, surcharge 12% above Rs. 1 crore

1% a month
