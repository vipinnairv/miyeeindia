One more item needs attention. A supplier invoice dated 10 May 2025 for goods of Rs. 3,00,000 (CGST Rs. 27,000, SGST Rs. 27,000) was taken as credit in the May 2025 return, filed on 20 June 2025. The supplier has not been paid because of a quality dispute.

45.2 Issues

  • Which tables of GSTR-3B carry each figure, and what is auto-populated.
  • Tax payable under reverse charge on legal fees and freight, and credit of that tax.
  • Blocked credit under section 17(5) on car repair and staff food.
  • Reversal under rule 37 for the unpaid supplier, with interest.
  • Set-off in the order laid down in section 49 and rule 88A, cash payable, and the 1% cash test of rule 86B.

45.3 Analysis and computation

Reverse charge. Legal services by an advocate or firm of advocates to a business entity are taxed in the hands of the recipient under section 9(3). Freight paid to a GTA that has not opted for forward charge is also taxed in the hands of a registered recipient at 5%. Both amounts are paid in cash; the ITC on them is available in the same return because the services are used for business. The 5% without-credit condition binds the GTA, not the recipient.

Blocked credit. Repair of a motor car used by a director is blocked by section 17(5)(ab) read with (a), since the car is not used for transport of goods, passengers or training. Food supplied to staff is blocked by section 17(5)(b)(i). Both are shown in Table 4A and reversed in Table 4B(1).

Rule 37. The 180th day from 10 May 2025 is 6 November 2025, which falls in November. The credit of Rs. 54,000 must be added back in the November return. It goes in Table 4B(2) because it can be reclaimed when the supplier is paid. Interest under section 50 is payable where the credit had been utilised. The company used its whole ledger every month, so interest runs from 21 June 2025 to 20 December 2025, 183 days.

Illustration 207: Rule 86B check for Suryoday Electricals

Step 1: Taxable value of outward supplies in the month is Rs. 95,00,000, which is more than Rs. 50 lakh. Rule 86B applies unless an exception is met.

Step 2: The income tax exception needs more than Rs. 1 lakh paid in each of the last two years for which the return time limit has expired. Rs. 62,000 and Rs. 88,000 do not qualify. No refund exception applies either.

Step 3: Minimum cash = 1% of output tax of Rs. 17,10,000 = Rs. 17,100.

Step 4: Cash actually paid against output tax = Rs. 4,47,500. Tax paid under reverse charge does not count because it is not output tax, but the condition is met anyway.

45.4 Compliance and documentation

  • GSTR-1 for November 2025 by 11 December 2025: B2B invoices invoice-wise, B2C inter-State invoices of value above the prescribed limit individually, other B2C in summary. Annual turnover is above Rs. 5 crore, so B2B invoices carry an IRN.
  • GSTR-3B by 20 December 2025. Table 3.1(a) is auto-populated from GSTR-1 and cannot be edited; errors are corrected in GSTR-1A before filing. Table 3.1(d) carries the reverse charge value and tax. Table 3.2 shows Rs. 2,00,000 supplied to unregistered persons in Rajasthan.
  • Self-invoice for the advocate fees and the GTA freight, and a payment voucher on payment.
  • A rule 37 register listing unpaid invoices by age. When the supplier is paid, the Rs. 54,000 is reclaimed in Table 4A(5) and also shown in Table 4D(1).

Key point

Lessons

Blocked credits are shown in Table 4A and reversed in 4B(1); rule 37 reversals go to 4B(2) so that the reclaim is traceable.

Count the 180 days from the invoice date, not from the date of receipt of goods, and build a monthly ageing report.

Rule 86B is tested every month on that month's value. A company that usually pays cash can still be caught in a month with large purchases.

Chapter summary

• Reverse charge on legal fees and GTA freight is paid in cash and taken as credit in the same return.

• Car repair and staff food are blocked under section 17(5) and reversed in Table 4B(1).

• The unpaid supplier triggered a rule 37 reversal of Rs. 54,000 with interest of Rs. 4,874.

• After set-off in the rule 88A order, cash payable for the month is Rs. 4,72,874.
