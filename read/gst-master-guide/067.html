Appendix E: Digest of Rulings

The table lists the rulings discussed in this guide, in the order in which they first appear. Rulings under the pre-GST laws (service tax, central excise, VAT and sales tax) are included where the principle carries over to GST. Court: SC is the Supreme Court and HC a High Court.

Case

Court and year

Principle

Relevant to

Commissioner of Central Excise v. Ratan Melting and Wire Industries

SC, 2008

Circulars bind the department but not the courts or taxpayers; a circular contrary to the statute has no legal effect

Interpretation; circulars

Commissioner of Customs v. Dilip Kumar and Co.

SC, 2018

Exemption notifications are construed strictly; burden on claimant; ambiguity resolved in favour of revenue

Interpretation; exemptions

Tata Consultancy Services v. State of Andhra Pradesh

SC, 2004

Canned software on a medium is goods; intangible property capable of being bought, sold, transmitted and stored can be goods

Definitions; goods and services

State of West Bengal v. Calcutta Club Ltd.

SC, 2019

Doctrine of mutuality applies to members' clubs, including incorporated clubs; no sales tax or service tax on supplies to members under the earlier law

Supply; clubs and associations

Indian Medical Association v. Union of India

Kerala HC, 2025

Sections 2(17)(e) and 7(1)(aa) with its Explanation declared unconstitutional for lack of legislative competence; Union's appeal pending in the Supreme Court

Supply; clubs and associations

GE T and D India Ltd. v. Deputy Commissioner of Central Excise

Madras HC, 2019

Notice pay recovered from employees is not consideration for any service of tolerating an act; not liable to service tax

Supply; employee recoveries

Bharat Sanchar Nigam Ltd. v. Union of India

SC, 2006

Composite contracts outside Article 366(29A) are tested by the dominant nature of the transaction; aspect theory cannot be used to split them

Composite and mixed supply

Larsen and Toubro Ltd. v. State of Karnataka

SC, 2013

Works contracts can be split to tax the goods element; dominant intention test does not apply; construction of flats under pre-completion agreements is a works contract

Composite and mixed supply; works contracts

Dunlop India Ltd. v. Union of India

SC, 1976

An article with a reasonable claim to a specific entry should not be put in a residuary entry; common commercial meaning governs tariff entries

Classification

HPL Chemicals Ltd. v. Commissioner of Central Excise

SC, 2006

Burden of proving the classification claimed by the department is on the revenue; residuary heading is the last resort

Classification

Material Recycling Association of India v. Union of India

Gujarat HC, 2020

Section 13(8)(b) of the IGST Act fixing place of supply of intermediary services at location of supplier upheld

Place of supply; intermediary

Dharmendra M. Jani v. Union of India

Bombay HC, 2023

After a split verdict, sections 13(8)(b) and 8(2) of the IGST Act upheld as valid

Place of supply; intermediary

Ernst and Young Ltd. v. Additional Commissioner, CGST Appeals-II, Delhi

Delhi HC, 2023

Services supplied on own account to overseas group firms are not intermediary services; separate legal entities are not establishments of the same person; export refund allowed

Intermediary; export of services

Union of India v. Cosmo Films Ltd.

SC, 2023

Pre-import condition for IGST exemption on imports under advance authorisation (13 October 2017 to 9 January 2019) upheld; relief through payment and refund or credit

Imports; advance authorisation

Bhandari Scrap Traders v. Union of India

SC, 2026

Section 16(2)(c) upheld; SLP against Gujarat High Court dismissed; VAT bona fide purchaser rulings do not carry over; credit restored when supplier pays

ITC; supplier default

Diya Agencies v. State Tax Officer

Kerala HC, 2023

Sections 16(2)(c) and 16(4) upheld; ITC is a concession subject to conditions

ITC; conditions and time limit

Thirumalakonda Plywoods v. Assistant Commissioner

Andhra Pradesh HC, 2023

Time limit in section 16(4) upheld as a valid condition

ITC; time limit

Arise India Ltd. v. Commissioner of Trade and Taxes

Delhi HC, 2017

Bona fide purchaser not to be denied credit for seller's default under Delhi VAT

ITC; bona fide purchaser (VAT)

Jayam and Co. v. Assistant Commissioner

SC, 2016

Input tax credit is a concession available only on the statutory conditions

ITC; nature of credit

Eicher Motors Ltd. v. Union of India

SC, 1999

Modvat credit validly taken is indefeasible and cannot be lapsed by a later rule

ITC; nature of credit

Collector of Central Excise v. Dai Ichi Karkaria Ltd.

SC, 1999

Credit validly taken is as good as tax paid

ITC; nature of credit

Union of India v. Bharti Airtel Ltd.

SC, 2021

GSTR-3B is self-assessed on own records; no rectification of earlier returns outside the scheme because GSTR-2A was not available

ITC; returns

Riddhi Siddhi Enterprises v. Commissioner of Goods and Services Tax

Delhi HC, 2024

Retrospective cancellation of registration cannot be mechanical; the order must record reasons and objective material for the earlier date

Registration; cancellation

Hindustan Steel Ltd. v. State of Orissa

SC, 1969

Penalty not automatic for technical or venial breach or bona fide belief

Penalty; e-way bill

Danish Hassan v. Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir

J&K and Ladakh HC, 2026

Seven-day limit for notice under section 129(3) is mandatory; notice issued after the limit vitiated detention proceedings

E-way bill; detention

GMG Electric v. Commissioner of State Tax

GSTAT, Ernakulam Bench, 2026

Order under section 129(3) passed beyond seven days of notice is void; limit mandatory

E-way bill; detention

Ranbaxy Laboratories Ltd. v. Union of India

SC, 2011

Interest on delayed refund runs from expiry of statutory period after application, not from order date

Refunds; interest

Mafatlal Industries Ltd. v. Union of India

SC, 1997

Refund only where burden of tax not passed on (unjust enrichment)

Refunds; unjust enrichment

Gujarat Chamber of Commerce and Industry v. Union of India

Gujarat HC, 2025 (SLP dismissed, SC, 2026)

Assignment of leasehold rights in land is akin to transfer of immovable property and not a supply of service

Real estate; Schedule III

BirlaNu Ltd. v. Union of India

Telangana HC, 2025

Rule 39(1)(a), so far as it required ISD credit to be distributed in the same month, held beyond section 20 (as it stood before 1 April 2025); audit report and show cause notice quashed

Audit; input service distributor

Pushpam Pharmaceuticals Co. v. Collector of Central Excise

SC, 1995

Suppression means deliberate withholding of information to evade duty, not mere omission or bona fide belief

Demands; extended period

Uniworth Textiles Ltd. v. Commissioner of Central Excise

SC, 2013

Mere non-payment of duty is not suppression or wilful misstatement; conscious withholding must be shown

Demands; extended period

Radha Krishan Industries v. State of Himachal Pradesh

SC, 2021

Provisional attachment is drastic; needs tangible material, the Commissioner's own opinion, hearing on objections and a reasoned order

Recovery; provisional attachment

Radhika Agarwal v. Union of India

SC, 2025

Power of arrest upheld subject to recorded reasons to believe, written grounds and anticipatory bail; no coercive recovery during search

Search; arrest

P.V. Ramana Reddy v. Union of India

Telangana HC, 2019

Arrest for offences under section 132 can be made before the tax liability is adjudicated

Search; arrest

Columbia Sportswear Co. v. Director of Income Tax

SC, 2012

An advance ruling can be challenged in a writ petition under Articles 226 and 227 (Income-tax Act; principle applied to GST rulings)

Advance rulings; writ

Assistant Commissioner of State Tax v. Commercial Steel Ltd.

SC, 2021

Writ is not a substitute for statutory appeal except for breach of fundamental rights or natural justice, lack of jurisdiction or vires challenge

Appeals; writ

Singh Enterprises v. Commissioner of Central Excise

SC, 2008

Appellate authority cannot condone delay beyond the period the statute allows

Appeals; limitation

Collector, Land Acquisition, Anantnag v. Mst. Katiji

SC, 1987

Sufficient cause for condonation of delay to be read liberally to advance substantial justice

Appeals; condonation

Union of India v. Dharmendra Textile Processors

SC, 2008

Civil penalty for breach of a statutory obligation does not need mens rea unless the provision requires it

Penalties; mens rea

Union of India v. Mohit Minerals (P) Ltd.

SC, 2022

GST Council recommendations are persuasive, not binding; IGST under reverse charge on ocean freight in CIF imports struck down as it offends section 8

Constitution; imports

Safari Retreats (P) Ltd. v. Chief Commissioner of CGST

SC, 2024

Plant or machinery in section 17(5)(d) to be tested by functionality; overcome by retrospective amendment in Finance Act, 2025

Input tax credit; blocked credits

Commissioner of Customs, Central Excise and Service Tax v. Northern Operating Systems (P) Ltd.

SC, 2022

Secondment of employees by an overseas group company is supply of manpower service by that company to the Indian entity

Supply; import of services

Kone Elevator India (P) Ltd. v. State of Tamil Nadu

SC, 2014

Supply and installation of lifts is a works contract, not a sale (Constitution Bench)

Composite and mixed supply; works contracts

Union of India v. VKC Footsteps India (P) Ltd.

SC, 2021

Rule 89(5) upheld; inverted duty refund limited to ITC on inputs, excluding input services

Rates; inverted duty refund

Union of India v. Intercontinental Consultants and Technocrats (P) Ltd.

SC, 2018

Reimbursable expenses could not be included in value of taxable service under service tax; under GST only genuine pure agent costs stay out

Value of supply

Commissioner of Service Tax v. Bhayana Builders (P) Ltd.

SC, 2018

Materials supplied free by the service recipient are not part of the gross amount charged

Value of supply; free supplies

Suncraft Energy (P) Ltd. v. Assistant Commissioner, State Tax

Calcutta HC, 2023

Credit not to be denied only for GSTR-2A mismatch without first proceeding against defaulting supplier; now subject to Bhandari Scrap Traders

ITC; supplier default

Additional Commissioner v. Safecon Lifesciences (P) Ltd.

SC, 2026

Revenue SLP dismissed; extended period cannot be invoked against buyer only because supplier registration was cancelled retrospectively, without proof of buyer's fraud

ITC; retrospective cancellation

Samay Alloys India (P) Ltd. v. State of Gujarat

Gujarat HC, 2022

Rule 86A permits blocking only of credit available in the ledger; no negative blocking (Revenue SLP in Union of India v. K.K. Alloys, against a similar Punjab and Haryana High Court ruling, dismissed by the Supreme Court in June 2026)

ITC; blocking of credit

On Quest Merchandising India (P) Ltd. v. Government of NCT of Delhi

Delhi HC, 2017

Section 9(2)(g) of Delhi VAT Act read down to protect bona fide purchasing dealers

ITC; bona fide purchaser (VAT)

State of Karnataka v. Ecom Gill Coffee Trading (P) Ltd.

SC, 2023

Burden on dealer to prove genuineness of purchase; invoices and cheque payment alone not enough (KVAT)

ITC; burden of proof

Assistant Commissioner (ST) v. Satyam Shivam Papers (P) Ltd.

SC, 2022

No presumption of evasion where e-way bill validity lapsed due to external factors; State's SLP dismissed with costs

E-way bill; detention

Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs v. Aberdare Technologies (P) Ltd.

SC, 2025

Bona fide clerical errors in GSTR-1 may be corrected after the time limit where no revenue loss; portal limits no bar

Returns; rectification

Directorate General of Goods and Services Tax Intelligence v. Gameskraft Technologies (P) Ltd.

SC, 2026

Online platforms where players stake money supply actionable claims in betting and gambling; 28% on full stake value upheld; 2023 amendments clarificatory

Online gaming; valuation

Ghanashyam Mishra and Sons (P) Ltd. v. Edelweiss Asset Reconstruction Co. Ltd.

SC, 2021

Approved resolution plan extinguishes all claims not in the plan, including Government dues

Insolvency; recovery

Commissioner of Central Excise v. Brindavan Beverages (P) Ltd.

SC, 2007

Show cause notice is the foundation of the case; vague notice is bad and the order cannot go beyond it

Demands; show cause notice

Armour Security (India) Ltd. v. Commissioner, CGST, Delhi East

SC, 2025

Section 6(2)(b) bars the other administration once one has issued a show cause notice on the same subject matter; summons and inquiry are not proceedings

Demands; parallel proceedings

Assistant Commissioner (CT) LTU v. Glaxo Smith Kline Consumer Health Care Ltd.

SC, 2020

High Court should not entertain a writ to bypass an appeal period, including the condonable period, that has expired

Appeals; limitation

Reckitt Benckiser India (P) Ltd. v. Union of India

Delhi HC, 2024

Section 171 and the anti-profiteering rules upheld; methodology depends on facts; individual orders open to challenge

Anti-profiteering
