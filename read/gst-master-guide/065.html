Appendix C: GST Rate Chart after GST 2.0

GST 2.0 took effect on 22 September 2025 on the recommendations of the 56th GST Council meeting held on 3 September 2025. The main slabs are now 5% (merit rate) and 18% (standard rate), with 40% for select demerit and luxury goods and services. The 12% and 28% slabs have largely gone. Compensation cess was removed on all goods other than tobacco from 22 September 2025, and on tobacco too from 1 February 2026.

Key point

This chart is a quick guide, not a substitute for the rate notifications. Rates attach to HSN or SAC entries, and many entries carry conditions (packaged or not, branded or not, with or without ITC). Verify the exact HSN entry and its conditions in the current notification before applying a rate.

Practice note

Where a supplier moved from 18% with ITC to 5% without ITC (for example a salon), ITC on stock and capital goods relating to the now lower-rate supply needs review from 22 September 2025. Document the working and the reversal, if any, in the September 2025 file.

Reverse charge summary

Reverse charge applies to notified supplies under section 9(3) of the CGST Act (and section 5(3) of the IGST Act), and to supplies from unregistered persons notified under section 9(4).
