Month-by-month planner for FY 2026-27

This planner adds the year-specific events to the routine dates above. The routine monthly dates (10th, 11th, 13th, 20th, 25th) are not repeated in every row.

Month

Key events

April 2026

13 Apr: GSTR-1 (QRMP) for Jan to Mar 2026. 18 Apr: CMP-08 for Q4. 22/24 Apr: GSTR-3B (QRMP) Q4. 25 Apr: ITC-04. 30 Apr: last date to opt into QRMP for Apr to Jun.

May 2026

Routine returns. Start FY 2025-26 annual return reconciliations.

June 2026

30 Jun: GSTR-4 for FY 2025-26 (composition taxpayers).

July 2026

13 Jul: GSTR-1 (QRMP) Q1. 18 Jul: CMP-08 Q1. 22/24 Jul: GSTR-3B (QRMP) Q1.

August 2026

Routine returns. Chase vendors with pending FY 2025-26 invoices not in GSTR-2B.

September 2026

30 Sep: date for rule 37A check (suppliers who have not filed GSTR-3B for FY 2025-26).

October 2026

13 Oct: GSTR-1 (QRMP) Q2. 18 Oct: CMP-08 Q2. 22/24 Oct: GSTR-3B (QRMP) Q2. 25 Oct: ITC-04 half-year (AATO above Rs. 5 crore).

November 2026

30 Nov: last date for FY 2025-26 ITC (section 16(4)), credit notes (section 34(2)), GSTR-1 corrections and rule 37A reversals.

December 2026

31 Dec: GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C for FY 2025-26.

January 2027

13 Jan: GSTR-1 (QRMP) Q3. 18 Jan: CMP-08 Q3. 22/24 Jan: GSTR-3B (QRMP) Q3. 31 Jan: last date to opt into QRMP for Jan to Mar.

February 2027

QRMP option window for Apr to Jun 2027 opens on 1 Feb.

March 2027

31 Mar: CMP-02 for FY 2027-28, LUT for FY 2027-28, GTA option for FY 2027-28.

Practice note

Keep one tracker for all GSTINs of the group, with the person responsible and the ARN. Late fees usually arise from a forgotten GSTIN in a smaller State.
