Appendix A: Section Map of the CGST Act, 2017

The CGST Act has 174 sections arranged in 21 chapters, followed by three Schedules. Several sections have been inserted since 2017 with letter suffixes (for example 11A, 74A, 128A and 148A), and a few have been omitted (for example sections 42, 43 and 43A on invoice matching). The chapter map below helps locate a provision quickly. The SGST Acts follow the same numbering, so the same map works for State law.

The three Schedules carry much of the law on the meaning of supply: Schedule I lists activities treated as supply even without consideration, Schedule II decides whether an activity is a supply of goods or of services, and Schedule III lists activities that are neither goods nor services.

Key point

SGST Acts follow the CGST numbering. Check that a notice is issued by the correct authority and cites both laws where both taxes are demanded.
