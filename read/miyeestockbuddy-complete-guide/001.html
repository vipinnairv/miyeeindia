How To Use This Guide

The twenty parts are ordered so that each one uses only what came before it. You can read straight through, or you can enter at the level you are already at.

A word about the order

The derivatives parts sit in the second half deliberately. Options are not an advanced version of shares, they are a different instrument with a clock attached, and almost every person who loses money in them skipped the parts that came first.

If you find yourself tempted to jump to Part 11 because the Greeks sound interesting, read Part 10.4 on intrinsic and time value first. It is four pages, and without it the Greeks are memorisation rather than understanding.
