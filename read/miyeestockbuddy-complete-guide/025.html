What it is

A free, offline capable stock analyser and portfolio manager for Indian, US and crypto markets. It runs entirely in your browser, with no login and no server holding your data.

This edition

Twenty parts, forty seven diagrams. Written to be read by someone with no background in finance and to still be useful to someone already trading options.

Corrections, questions and suggestions are genuinely welcome. If something in these pages is wrong, unclear, or has been overtaken by a rule change, write to miyee.india@gmail.com and it will be fixed in the next edition.

If you found this useful

Pass it on to somebody who is about to start. The parts that would have saved them the most money are Part 16 on position sizing and Part 20 on the mistakes, and almost nobody reads those first.

Important disclaimer

This guide is educational material only. It is not investment advice, and it is not a recommendation to buy or sell any security, index or derivative contract.

MiyeeStock Buddy and its author, Vipin Nair, are not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser or as a Research Analyst. Company and index names appear only as familiar illustrations of a concept, never as recommendations. All prices, premiums, lot sizes, margins, tax rates and expiry arrangements used in the examples are illustrative and change over time. Verify the current contract specifications on the exchange website and the current tax position with a qualified professional before acting.

Indicators, patterns, ratios and option Greeks describe what has already happened and what is currently priced. They do not predict the future. Markets can and do behave in ways no model anticipated.

Derivatives carry the risk of losing substantially more than the amount you deposit. SEBI’s own studies have repeatedly found that a large majority of individual traders in equity derivatives lose money. Treat that as the base rate, not as a warning meant for other people.

Every investment decision, and every consequence of it, is yours alone. Please consult a SEBI registered adviser before acting, and never invest money you cannot afford to lose.
