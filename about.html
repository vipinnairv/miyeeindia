MiyeeIndia Tech Labs is an independent lab creating lightweight finance and productivity tools.

Our story

MiyeeIndia Tech Labs was started by Vipin Nair to make professional-grade finance and document tools accessible to small businesses, finance teams and individual investors in India.

Every product solves one problem well - compliance-ready accounting, disciplined stock analysis, CFO-level reporting, and dependable PDF handling - without bloated setup or heavy cost.

Meet the founder

Vipin Nair

Vipin Nair is the founder of MiyeeIndia Tech Labs, based in Ahmedabad. He builds the lab's tools himself, from accounting and stock analysis to CFO dashboards and PDF processing, with a focus on Indian compliance and finance workflows.

He is also the author of two professional reference guides, Goods and Services Tax: The Professional's Master Guide and Income-tax Act, 2025: The Professional's Master Guide, both available as free downloads.
