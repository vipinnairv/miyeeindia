Miyee Task Manager Pro

A comprehensive task and project management web app, developed by Vipin Nair, to track personal goals or manage team collaboration. Free to use, to help the MSME business community grow.

Launch app ↗ All products
Key use cases

Work solo or as a team

Personal mode

A streamlined setup to track your individual goals, projects and daily tasks.

Company / Team mode

A collaborative workspace where an Admin sets up the organisation hierarchy (for example Director, Manager, Executive), assigns roles, and invites team members using unique access codes.

Core features

Everything in one workspace

Command Dashboard

A high-level view of your productivity: total tasks, completion rates, upcoming deadlines, priority distribution and a workflow pipeline.

My Day and Calendar

A focused view of what needs your attention today, plus a full calendar to manage dated tasks.

Goals and Projects

Define short, medium and long-term goals with time budgets, then break them down into actionable projects.

Advanced task management

Status (To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done), priority (Low, Medium, High), tags and projects, assignment to team members, subtasks, recurring tasks and comments.

Admin Console

For team workspaces: manage workspace settings, define job positions, control user permissions and generate invite codes.

Data and integrations

Your data stays portable

Backup and restore

Download or restore your entire workspace as a JSON file.

Exports

Export tasks to an Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx) or as calendar files (.ics) for Apple Calendar or Outlook.

Google Calendar sync

Two-way synchronisation with Google Calendar through OAuth.
