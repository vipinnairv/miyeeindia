Two free master guides on GST and the Income-tax Act, 2025

Two professional reference guides by Vipin Nair are now available on the Free Downloads page. Both are First Edition, dated 28 September 2026.

Goods and Services Tax: The Professional's Master Guide

Covers GST 2.0 and the law as amended by the Finance Act, 2026. It runs from supply, valuation and place of supply through input tax credit, registration, returns and refunds, to sector topics, assessment, appeals, accounting and reconciliation, with worked illustrations and case studies.

Income-tax Act, 2025: The Professional's Master Guide

Covers Tax Year 2026-27, as amended by the Finance Act, 2026. It shows the new section beside the old one and covers individuals, business and profession, capital gains, corporate tax, mergers, international taxation, transfer pricing, and TDS and compliance.

A note on use

The guides are for study and general reference and are not legal or tax advice. Rates, thresholds and due dates change through notifications, so check official sources before acting.
