Free guides on GST, income tax, Ind AS and markets are now in the Library

The new Library brings together five reference guides, free to read in your browser.

What is in the Library

  • Goods and Services Tax: The Professional's Master Guide. Covers GST 2.0 and the law as amended by the Finance Act, 2026.
  • Income-tax Act, 2025: The Professional's Master Guide. Covers Tax Year 2026-27, with the new section beside the old one.
  • Technical Accounting Manual: IFRS, IAS and Ind AS Equivalents. Standard-by-standard analysis with carve-outs.
  • Comprehensive Guide to Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS). A deep dive for beginners and professionals.
  • MiyeeStockBuddy: The Complete Guide to Indian Markets. From the first share to futures and options, as education only.

Read online only

The guides are provided for reading on this site and are not available to download. They are for general reference and are not legal, tax or investment advice. Rates, thresholds and due dates change through notifications, so check official sources before acting.
